Privacy, in plain English

DRAFT — [ASH: have this reviewed before publishing]. This page describes how the Chess Mates app and website work today. If the app changes (accounts, online play, anything new), this page must be updated first.

Chess Mates teaches chess to kids, so we keep things very simple: we collect as little as possible — and for the app today, that means basically nothing.

The Chess Mates app

  • Progress and profiles stay on the device. Lesson progress, puzzle results, games against the bots and any profiles created in the app are stored on the phone or tablet the app is installed on. They are not sent to us.
  • No online accounts. There is nothing to sign up for. Kids can set up a profile in the app — a name or nickname, an avatar, and an optional PIN — but it lives only on the device, is never sent to us, and needs no sign-up.
  • No ads. The app shows no advertising of any kind.
  • No third-party tracking. No analytics trackers, no advertising SDKs, no selling or sharing of data — there is no data to sell or share.
  • Deleting the app deletes the data. Because progress lives only on the device, removing the app removes it.
  • Using the app in a browser. If you use the app in a browser at chessmates.online/app, the same progress and profile data is kept in your browser's local storage on your device instead — clearing the site's data in your browser removes it. It is still never sent to us.

This website

  • The site is plain pages — no cookies set by us, no analytics trackers, no ad networks. (The app at chessmates.online/app keeps its data in your browser's local storage, as described above — still nothing sent to us.)
  • If you email us at info@chessmates.com.au, we'll have your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it to reply and to handle whatever you asked about (like arranging classes). We don't add you to mailing lists or share your details, and we'll delete the exchange if you ask.
