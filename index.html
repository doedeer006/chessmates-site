The online program

Learn it. Practise it. Play it.

The whole journey lives in the app — from "which one is the rook?" to real tactics — one friendly loop, hundreds of chess-engine-checked puzzles.

Learn

A guided path with the King first: stories, songs and lessons for every piece, then castling, check, checkmate and real tactics.

Practise

Puzzle Play with its own rating — start at 100, solve puzzles served at exactly your level, and watch your Puzzle Power climb.

Play

Four bots from beatable Pawn Pete to champion Queen Quinn — with hints, take-backs, and kind words when you lose.

Made for families

A profile for every kid in the house — each with their own progress, badges to earn, and a parent corner so grown-ups can see it all.

Safe by design

No ads, no chat, no strangers. Progress stays on your device. Read the privacy policy — it's short and in plain English.

Off the screen

We teach in real life too

The online program grew out of our classes — Chess Mates coaches teach in schools and outside school hours care (OSHC) programs, where kids move through three levels at their own pace:

  • Bronze First moves — meet the pieces, learn how each one travels, and play your very first full games.
  • Silver Growing confidence — simple tactics, looking after your pieces, and winning (and losing) like a good sport.
  • Gold Big-brain chess — plans, combinations and real match play for kids ready to be challenged.
